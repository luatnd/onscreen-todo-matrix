How to read the colors

R = urgency, G = ease (1 − effort), B = value. Each channel runs from 55 to 255. Brightness ≈ priority; hue = why.

  • ● White #FFF: urgent, valuable, easy. Do now.
  • ● Magenta: urgent, valuable, hard. Big and pressing.
  • ● Cyan: valuable, easy, not urgent. A quick win you can schedule.
  • ● Yellow: urgent, easy, low value. Someone else's fire, so delegate it.
  • ● Blue: valuable, hard, not urgent. A major project.
  • ● Green: easy, low value, not urgent. A fill-in.
  • ● Dark #373737: hard, low value, not urgent. Thankless, so drop it.

What changed vs v1

  • v3d: stems back up to ~45% opacity (solid, 1.2px), between v1 and v2. Labels stacked in the empty top-right, so the floor uses the full width.
  • v3: floor ~40% larger and tilted more toward you (pitch 38°), urgency height squeezed to 55% of floor size, 10×10 grid at low opacity with the quadrant split lines brighter.
  • Stems: thin, dashed, ~20% opacity; floor shadow is a faint smudge. They hint at position without competing with the dots.
  • Camera: looks straight down the diagonal from the "Quick wins" corner toward the origin. Stronger perspective, so near tasks are drawn bigger and far ones shrink and fade.
  • Depth fade: far dots are dimmer, so your eye lands on the front first.
  • Labels: labels for the top 3 and on-fire tasks are spread apart with thin leader lines so they no longer overlap.
