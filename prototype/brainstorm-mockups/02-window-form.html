C. Hybrid: large overlay by default, can be pinned as a floating panel (recommended)

The shortcut opens a large centered overlay for the 2-second glance (B). One key (⌘P) pins it, so it shrinks into the always-on-top floating panel (A) and stays while you work. There's a menu-bar icon and no Dock icon. The app remembers which mode you used last.
