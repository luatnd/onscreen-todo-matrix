A. Chart-first (recommended)

The chart fills the panel. A thin top bar has the saved-filter dropdown (JQL presets), last sync time, refresh and settings. A one-line Next strip sits at the bottom. Hovering a dot shows a tooltip, and clicking shows a detail card (shown above) with ⌘O Open in Jira. Nothing else takes space away from the chart.

B. Chart + sidebar

A right sidebar shows Next 3, On fire and quick project filters. The ◧ button or ⌘\ collapses it. Lists are easier to scan than labels in 3D, but the chart loses about 27% of its width in an already small panel.

C. Tall panel: chart on top, ranked list below

A narrow, tall panel you can dock at the screen edge. The chart on top shows the shape, and a list ranked by score, colored the same as the dots, shows the order. Selecting a row highlights its dot and the other way round. This works well beside a code editor, but the chart is the smallest of the three.

Click a card to pick, then reply in the terminal. Combinations are fine, for example "A, but with the C list toggleable".
